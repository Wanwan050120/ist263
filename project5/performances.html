How I Practice

I usually begin by dividing a song into smaller sections. I practice difficult phrases slowly, focus on clean timing, and then connect the sections as the performance becomes more comfortable. Recording myself helps me notice uneven rhythm, unwanted string noise, and changes in dynamics that are easy to miss while I am playing.
