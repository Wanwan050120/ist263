Music and Me

Welcome to my personal music portfolio. I am Haotian Chen, a student and guitar player who enjoys exploring how music can express ideas and emotions that are difficult to communicate with words alone. This website brings together my story, my guitar performances, and the music that inspires me.

My Connection to Music

Playing guitar gives me a reason to listen more carefully. I pay attention to rhythm, tone, phrasing, and the small details that make one performance feel different from another. Learning a song can be challenging, but practicing it section by section makes the final performance rewarding.
