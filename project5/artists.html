Artist Recommendations

The artists on this page approach guitar music in different ways. Listening to their work helps me discover new sounds and think more carefully about technique, composition, tone, and my own musical identity.

Plini

Plini is an Australian guitarist and composer whose instrumental music combines progressive rock, melodic guitar writing, and atmospheric arrangements. I enjoy how his music balances technical playing with memorable melodies.

Recommended song: Electric Sunrise

Listen to Plini on Spotify

Polyphia

Polyphia combines instrumental rock, progressive music, hip-hop-influenced rhythms, and modern production. Their work shows how guitar can function as both a melodic and rhythmic instrument.

Recommended song: Playing God

Listen to Polyphia on Spotify

Modern Day Babylon

Modern Day Babylon is an instrumental progressive metal band known for combining heavy guitar tones, precise rhythms, and ambient melodic sections. Their music demonstrates how technical playing can support a strong atmosphere.

Recommended song: Travelers

Listen to Modern Day Babylon on Spotify
